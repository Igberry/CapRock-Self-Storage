Rentals run month to month unless your signed Rental Agreement states a fixed term.

Gate hours are 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, seven days a week. Office hours are 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM Monday to Saturday, and 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM on Sunday. Please call the office before visiting, as staffing can vary.

You receive an individual access credential at move-in. Do not share it with anyone who is not named on your Rental Agreement's authorised access list. You must give us at least 30 days' written notice before your intended move-out date. Rent and charges continue until you complete your move-out duties or we accept surrender of the space in writing.

The facility

Our Lubbock property offers:

  • Storage units from a 5 × 5 up to a 20 × 40, plus outdoor vehicle parking
  • Temperature controlled interior units and drive-up outdoor units
  • Powered and lighted units
  • Gated entry with electronic gate access
  • Video surveillance and a fenced and walled perimeter
  • Carts and dollies, boxes and moving supplies on site
  • Low cost insurance available, and deliveries accepted

Not every feature applies to every unit. The features of the specific unit you rent are those described in your Rental Agreement.

Facility rules

To keep the property safe, every tenant agrees to:

  • Never store anything illegal, hazardous, flammable, explosive or perishable, or anything likely to attract pests
  • Never sleep, live or run a business out of a unit. A unit is not a residence, workshop, office or place of business
  • Keep the unit locked with an approved lock whenever you are not present
  • Never sublet, assign or share a unit with anyone not named on the Rental Agreement
  • Follow posted access instructions, drive slowly on site, and never tailgate through the gate
  • Report damage, break-ins or suspicious activity to facility staff straight away
  • Never alter the unit, or use facility equipment as stored property

The full list of prohibited property and facility rules is set out in your Rental Agreement and its exhibits.

Default and lien rights

Your Rental Agreement contains a contractual landlord's lien on property stored at the facility, securing rent, fees and other charges you owe us. That lien, and any enforcement of it, is governed by Chapter 59 of the Texas Property Code and other applicable law.

In general terms, before any property is sold, towed or otherwise disposed of to satisfy a lien, we follow the notice, waiting period and sale procedures Chapter 59 requires, including delivering a written notice of claim and observing the statutory waiting periods before advertising or selling. You may redeem your property at any point before sale by paying the amount owed plus the reasonable expenses we incurred enforcing the lien.

This is a summary. The complete default and lien procedure, including the fee schedule, notice methods and your rights, is set out in your signed Rental Agreement, which controls.
