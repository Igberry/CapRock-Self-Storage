Let’s get started

Storage for life’s big and small moments

Most people do not rent a storage unit because they want to. They rent one because a lease ended, a house sold, a kid came home for the summer, or the garage finally lost. Whatever put you here, the unit itself should be the simplest part.

That is why leases run month to month. Take the space for a season or for a few years, and hand back the keys when you are done. If you are not sure how much room you need, the size guide lays out all nine sizes side by side, and the storage calculator turns a rough description of what you are storing into a starting size.

Why customers choose CapRock

No long contracts, no guessing about who can get onto the property, and no mystery about what you are renting.

  • Month-to-month leases — no long-term commitment, so you can leave when your situation changes.
  • Gated access — entry to the property is controlled, not open to anyone who drives past.
  • Video surveillance — cameras cover the property.
  • Clean, well-maintained buildings — the grounds and units are looked after.
  • Nine unit sizes — from a 2 × 5 locker up to a 10 × 30, so you are not paying for space you will not use.
  • Vehicle and RV parking — options vary by location, so ask your nearest facility what they currently offer.

If you know roughly what you need, find your nearest location and give them a call.
