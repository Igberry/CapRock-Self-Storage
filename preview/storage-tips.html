Storage Tips

A few habits that make packing, storing, and finding things later a lot easier.

01

Label every box

Write the room and a few contents on at least two sides of each box, so you can read it no matter how it's stacked.

02

Use uniform box sizes where you can

Same-sized boxes stack more securely and use space more efficiently than a mix of odd shapes.

03

Heavier items on the bottom

Stack heavier, sturdier boxes first and lighter or fragile items on top to avoid crushing.

04

Leave a walkway

Plan an aisle down the middle of your unit so you can reach items in the back without unstacking everything.

05

Cover furniture with breathable covers

Cloth sheets or moving blankets protect from dust while still letting air circulate. Plastic can trap moisture.

06

Keep items off the floor

Pallets or a few spare boards under boxes and furniture help protect against ground moisture.

07

Disassemble large furniture

Bed frames, tables, and shelving units usually store more compactly, and safely, taken apart.

08

Keep an inventory

A simple list or a few photos of what's in each box can save a lot of guesswork later.
