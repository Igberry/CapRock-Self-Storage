Vehicle Storage

A secure place to park your car, motorcycle, RV, or boat when you don't have room for it at home.

Why store your vehicle with CapRock

Street and driveway parking leaves vehicles exposed to weather and passersby. A dedicated storage space keeps a car, motorcycle, RV, or boat off your property and under watch, without tying up space you need at home.

Options vary by location

Depending on the facility, vehicle storage may include uncovered parking spaces, covered parking, or enclosed units large enough for a vehicle. Ask your nearest CapRock location which options they currently offer.

Flexible, month-to-month

Storage terms run month-to-month, so you're not locked into a long-term contract for something you might only need seasonally.
